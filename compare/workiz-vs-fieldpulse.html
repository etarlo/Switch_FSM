When Workiz is the better choice

Workiz is purpose-built for small-to-mid field service businesses that need reliable scheduling, dispatching, and invoicing without enterprise complexity. If any of these sound like you, Workiz is worth a serious look:

  • You handle high-volume inbound calls and need built-in VoIP with call tracking
  • Your business is dispatch-heavy with same-day on-demand jobs (locksmith, junk removal)
  • You want robust SMS/text job communication baked into the platform
  • A proven track record with 120,000+ customers matters to you
  • You value a large marketplace of integrations and Zapier automations

When FieldPulse makes more sense

FieldPulse brings a strong CRM-first approach to field service management. If your business leans on sales workflows and longer projects, FieldPulse's feature set may be a better fit:

  • You need a built-in CRM with a visual sales pipeline for estimates and follow-ups
  • Route optimization is important for your team's daily scheduling
  • Your business handles longer projects (construction, installs) alongside service calls
  • FieldPulse's flat-rate pricebook and proposals workflow fit your sales process
  • You want more advanced custom reporting and dashboards
